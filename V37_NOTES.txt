SYSY FAMILY iPhone App - V37

V37 focuses on safer customer management:
- Added duplicate-customer detection by normalized name or phone digits when creating a customer.
- Added an in-form duplicate warning before a new customer is saved.
- Added delete confirmation to prevent accidental customer deletion.
- Delete warning explicitly explains that related quotation, payment and measurement history is removed together.
- Existing customer search, edit flow and account history remain intact.
- V36 backup/restore validation and checksum support are preserved.

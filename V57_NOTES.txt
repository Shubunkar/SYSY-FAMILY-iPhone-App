SYSY FAMILY iPhone App - V57

Advance-date accounting accuracy:
- Added an optional persisted advanceDate to BusinessQuotation so monthly collection uses the date the advance was recorded instead of always using quotation creation date.
- New quotations with an advance stamp the advance date automatically.
- Editing a quotation keeps the existing advance date when the advance is unchanged, updates it when the advance amount changes, and clears it when advance becomes zero.
- Older backups remain backward compatible: missing advanceDate falls back to createdAt for quotations that already have an advance.
- Preserved V56 backup version/filename consistency, checksum/restore validation, accounting totals, customer history, quotation filtering and payment safeguards.

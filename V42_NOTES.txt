SYSY FAMILY iPhone App - V42

V42 quotation management improvements:
- Added a fast status filter bar to the quotation list.
- Filters include All plus Draft, Sent, Confirmed, Production, Installed and Completed.
- Each status filter shows its live quotation count.
- Status filtering works together with quotation/customer/phone search.
- Tapping the active status filter clears it for quick switching.
- Preserved V41 persistent quotation status workflow, PDF status, editing, deletion confirmation, backup checksum validation and backward compatibility.
